# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Project: NEVORA

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Owner: Ariyan Hosseini

---

# 1. Executive Summary

NEVORA is a premium digital coffee brand.

The goal is not only to sell coffee online, but to create an immersive, cinematic, and emotionally engaging experience around specialty coffee.

The website should combine elegant design, storytelling, performance, and modern engineering to deliver a world-class user experience.

---

# 2. Product Vision

Create the most visually memorable coffee website possible.

Every interaction should communicate quality, craftsmanship, and trust.

The website must feel closer to Apple, Aesop, and Blue Bottle Coffee than to a traditional e-commerce store.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Most coffee websites focus only on product listings and pricing.

They fail to create emotional engagement or a premium browsing experience.

Customers should remember the brand experience—not just the products.

---

# 4. Goals

Primary Goals

- Build a premium digital brand.
- Increase user engagement.
- Drive online coffee sales.
- Showcase coffee craftsmanship.
- Create an unforgettable homepage.

Secondary Goals

- Improve SEO.
- Support future scaling.
- Enable content marketing.
- Support international expansion.

---

# 5. Non Goals

Version 1 will NOT include:

- Mobile application
- Marketplace
- Wholesale portal
- Loyalty program
- AI recommendation engine
- Multi-language support
- Subscription service

These will be implemented in future phases.

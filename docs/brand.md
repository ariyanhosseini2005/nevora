# NEVORA Brand Guidelines

Version: 1.0

---

# Brand Overview

Brand Name

NEVORA

Industry

Premium Specialty Coffee

Positioning

A luxury coffee brand focused on experience, craftsmanship, and modern design.

NEVORA is not simply selling coffee.

It is selling moments.

The essence of NEVORA can be described in four words:

Craft

Luxury

Warmth

Experience

Every product, interaction, and visual element should reinforce these ideas.

# Writing Style

Use short sentences.

Avoid unnecessary marketing words.

Focus on emotions before features.

Describe experiences instead of products.

Bad Example

"The best coffee with amazing quality and low prices."

Good Example

"Crafted from carefully selected beans, roasted to reveal every delicate note."

Luxury

# Typography Personality

Headings should feel editorial.

Body text should feel effortless to read.

Typography should create elegance through spacing rather than decoration.

---

# Iconography

Use only outline icons.

Simple.

Modern.

Consistent stroke width.

Avoid colorful icons.

Avoid skeuomorphic icons.

---

# Motion Personality

Animations should feel:

Natural

Slow

Intentional

Elegant

Motion must support storytelling.

Never animate for decoration only.

---

# Customer Perception

After leaving the website, users should remember:

The atmosphere.

The coffee.

The quality.

The design.

The experience.

Not the discounts.

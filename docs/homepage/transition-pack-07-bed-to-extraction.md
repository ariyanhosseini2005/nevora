## Frame-by-frame transition logic

### Frames 568–579 — Finish particle accumulation

- Particle flow from Transition Pack 06 decreases naturally.
- Grounds spread inside the circular basket without becoming instantly level.
- Camera descends with the last material and remains near the central axis.
- No tamper, water, wet patch, or liquid is visible yet.

### Frames 580–591 — Level, compress, and prepare the pore

- A clean circular mechanical tamper compresses the bed without a person or hand.
- Tamper movement remains vertical and aligned to the basket.
- As the tamper lifts, the camera glides close to the dry granular surface.
- One central dark pore becomes the target and expands toward lens ownership.

### Frames 592–599 — Cross through coffee texture

- Surrounding granules and the central pore cover the full lens.
- Camera scale crosses from surface macro to the filter underside while hidden.
- The pore position remains fixed and becomes the first underside extraction channel.
- No visible dissolve, circular wipe, or unrelated metal occlusion is allowed.

### Frames 600–607 — Saturate before extraction

- Pressurized water arrives above the puck and advances from upper-left to lower-right.
- Dry grounds darken spatially; they do not change all at once.
- Multiple tiny translucent beads form at real basket holes.
- No continuous stream may appear until sufficient visible saturation exists.

### Frames 608–623 — Coalesce into the hero stream

- Small beads grow under surface tension and begin connecting.
- One central primary stream forms while two smaller threads remain subordinate.
- Liquid shifts from dark wet coffee to translucent amber at thin edges.
- Camera begins a controlled downward follow on the primary stream axis.

## Optical and material rules

- Lens language stays near 100 mm macro through the camera scale transition.
- Dry bed remains visibly granular after tamping; it cannot resemble a smooth disk.
- Mechanical tamper is brushed steel with only its lower circular edge visible.
- Saturation needs progressive wetting, channel formation, and micro-bubble growth.
- Espresso must show real viscosity, translucent edges, and restrained crema bubbles.
- Upper-left light direction and circular portafilter geometry remain fixed.

## Rejection conditions

- Invisible or magical tamping, a human hand, or a new barista scene.
- Teleport from top of the bed to below the portafilter.
- Clear water, flooding, premature liquid, or instant full saturation.
- Perfectly smooth synthetic coffee bed or mirror-chrome steel.
- Thick syrup blob, chaotic jets, latte art, steam wall, glass, or cup.
- Camera-axis jump, light flip, crossfade, or unrelated underside composition.

# NEVORA — One-Take Grayscale Animatic v1

Status: Interactive previs ready for continuity review  
Production state: Design only; final image production and website implementation remain frozen

## Purpose

This pass is not final photography. It is a low-detail motion test for the exact
720-frame timing map. Its only job is to prove that the camera path, hero-object
continuity, scene geography, and transformation logic read as one unbroken take.

## What this animatic locks

- One continuous camera axis from farm entry to espresso glass.
- One traceable hero cherry/seed/bean throughout the journey.
- The farmer's hand appears only during frames 120–179.
- Basket occlusion owns the farm-to-processing environment change.
- The processing waterline physically becomes the drying-bed edge.
- The drying-bed rails lead into the circular roaster opening.
- The bean crease aligns with the grinder before the scale transition.
- Ground particles carry the camera into the portafilter.
- The first espresso stream remains continuous into the final glass.
- The camera pulls back for the first time only during frames 672–719.

## Review criteria

The animatic is approved only if all five statements are true:

1. A viewer can name the production stage without reading a caption.
2. The hero object never teleports between screen positions.
3. Reverse scrubbing remains understandable and physically plausible.
4. No environment change feels like a dissolve between unrelated still images.
5. The final pullback feels like a release after sustained macro proximity.

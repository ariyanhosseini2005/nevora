## Frame-by-frame transition logic

### Frames 328–339 — Converge the rails

- The seed continues forward-right from Transition Pack 02.
- The illuminated wooden rail narrows toward a real circular opening.
- Camera height remains aligned with the hero seed center.
- Background drying seeds lose focus and visual priority as the drum grows.

### Frames 340–347 — Prepare the tangent

- The wooden highlight approaches the lower-left steel arc.
- The seed rotates gradually toward the lip; its seam does not reset.
- Patinated metal replaces wood only where the physical surfaces meet.
- The opening must read as industrial depth, never as a graphic circle or portal.

### Frames 348–355 — Cross the threshold

- The seed rolls from wood onto the lower steel lip under real contact and weight.
- At least four frames show the seed shared by exterior light and interior shadow.
- The camera follows through the same opening slightly behind the hero.
- The lens transition begins from 85 mm toward 65 mm without a field-of-view jump.

### Frames 356–368 — Transfer light to the rim

- The circular rim expands beyond most frame edges as the camera passes inside.
- Exterior upper-left light becomes a thin crescent on the inner rim.
- Perforated steel and restrained dust establish the drum interior.
- The hero seed remains pale green; heat transformation has not started.

## Optical and material rules

- Lens language transitions smoothly from 85 mm close macro to 65 mm inside the drum.
- Apparent scale may grow only from physical camera approach.
- Hero rotation change across the pack should remain under 18 degrees.
- Wood needs sun age, grain, drying moisture, and worn edge detail.
- Steel requires patina, micro-scratches, bolts, thickness, and perforation depth.
- Interior darkness is real depth with recoverable texture, not a fade-to-black.

## Rejection conditions

- A circular portal, graphic iris transition, or unrelated dark tunnel.
- A seed floating through the opening without touching the lip.
- Instant change from pale seed to roasted brown bean.
- Scale jump, rotation reset, camera-height jump, or reversed direction.
- Flames, sparks, glowing embers, smoke wall, or dramatic fire spectacle.
- Duplicate hero seed or sharp background seeds inside the drum.

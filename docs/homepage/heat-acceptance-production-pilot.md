This slice begins at the cold-drum F396 boundary. The same hero seed enters a

background beans, abrupt color patch, oil, char, or identity replacement is

## Generation method

The F408 and F444 anchors and four 12-frame production sheets were created with
the built-in image-generation flow. Prompts locked the same hero identity,
clockwise drum/camera co-orbit, fixed upper-left light, low physical tumble,
monotonic color/material change, sparse transparent vapor, and the prohibition
on duplicate beans, background beans, fire, smoke walls, oil, char, text, logos,
people, cups, and grinders.

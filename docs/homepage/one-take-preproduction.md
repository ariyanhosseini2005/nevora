# NEVORA — One-Take Preproduction

Status: Storyboard direction v1  
Production state: Design only; implementation is frozen

## Locked creative decisions

- The complete journey is perceived as one continuous camera take.
- The camera begins inside a living coffee farm and ends on one espresso shot.
- No conventional cut, slideshow transition, or unrelated image dissolve is allowed.
- Every processing stage must remain recognizable to a non-expert viewer.
- One hero cherry/seed/bean carries the narrative through the whole film.
- A farmer's hand appears only during the harvest moment; no face is shown.
- Light always enters from the upper-left so every environment feels connected.

## Continuous camera path

### 01 — Enter the origin

The camera moves forward at leaf height through wet arabica plants before
sunrise. A foreground leaf briefly brushes across the lens to establish depth.

Transition ownership: the camera movement itself leads directly to the cherry.

### 02 — Discover the cherry

Forward movement decelerates and focus settles on one ripe crimson cherry. The
same cherry becomes the hero object for the rest of the journey.

Transition ownership: the branch stays fixed while the hand enters from shadow.

### 03 — Human selection

Only fingertips are visible. They make contact, apply a gentle twist, and free
the cherry without shaking the full plant.

Transition ownership: the camera follows the released cherry downward.

### 04 — Fall into the basket

The cherry falls into a woven harvest basket. The camera follows close behind
until the basket weave surrounds the full frame.

Transition ownership: basket darkness creates a natural lens occlusion, not a cut.

### 05 — Reveal the seed

The darkness gains moving highlights and resolves into processing water. Cherry
skin opens in front of the camera and reveals the pale green seed inside.

Transition ownership: the water current becomes the camera's new movement path.

### 06 — Water to drying

The camera follows the same seed as water carries it toward a bright edge. It
slides onto the drying bed without changing screen direction.

Transition ownership: the wooden bed edge forms a visual rail into the roaster.

### 07 — Enter the roaster

The seed travels along the drying surface and enters the circular darkness of
the roaster drum. The camera follows through the opening.

Transition ownership: the drum opening replaces the whole frame before the heat appears.

### 08 — Transformation by heat

The camera orbits with the same seed inside the moving drum. Its color and
surface develop continuously from green through cinnamon to roasted brown.

Transition ownership: the final rotation aligns the bean crease with the grinder.

### 09 — Enter the grind

The roasted bean moves directly toward the grinder burrs. The camera approaches
the central crease and crosses the scale boundary with it.

Transition ownership: the bean fractures around the lens and becomes grounds.

### 10 — Settle in the portafilter

The camera descends with the grounds into a bottomless portafilter. Particles
settle and the surface becomes an even prepared bed.

Transition ownership: the camera stops beneath the bed as water arrives above.

### 11 — Become the extraction

Water passes through the grounds. The camera travels with the first viscous
espresso stream and exits through the underside of the portafilter.

Transition ownership: the stream provides an unbroken line into the glass.

### 12 — Complete the ritual

The camera pulls back for the first time. The full espresso glass becomes
visible, the stream stops, crema settles, and the movement comes to rest.

The NEVORA brand reveal happens only after the surface is calm.

## Rules for the next design pass

- Build a low-detail animatic before any final photorealistic frame production.
- Verify that the hero object never teleports or changes direction without cause.
- Every transition must share shape, motion, light, or physical space.
- Do not add copy, navigation, or commerce UI to the animatic yet.
- Test the camera path in forward and reverse order because scroll controls both.
